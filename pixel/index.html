PIXEL

You are one black pixel. The maze is one pixel wide. The wall around the world is black and solid, and one single white opening in it is the way out.

Rules

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Move into a wall and you die. Move out of the field and you die. There is one exception: a single pixel of the wall is missing, and moving into that one wins.

You get one life. Ranked runs use a daily seed, so everybody faces the same maze on the same day — one attempt per mode per day. Free Play is random, unranked and unlimited.

You are drawn in exactly the colour of the walls. The blink is the only thing that tells you apart from the maze: you flicker black and white, between a wall that is not there and an opening that is not there.

EASY — brutal but fair

You move one pixel per keypress and can stop and think for as long as you like. The whole field is visible. Nothing kills you that you did not do yourself. The exit is marked green.

MEDIUM — fair core, cruel garnish

You cannot stop. The pixel runs, follows corridors on its own, and speeds up as the run goes on. The exit is not marked — it is one white opening in a black wall, and there are other openings that are not it. Those cost you time, not your life. There is no pause, and leaving the window ends the run.

HARD — actively hostile

You see a 70-pixel circle around yourself and nothing else. A tone rises in pitch as you get closer to the true exit. The maze fills in its own dead ends every few seconds, and being caught in one kills you. Turning into a wall kills you instantly — there is no grace.

Your controls are reassigned at random. The current mapping is always shown in the bottom right. You are never lied to about it. You just have to be looking.

Some gaps in the wall are not the exit, and in this mode they kill.
